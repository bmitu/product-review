Hestan TITUM Fusion Nonstick Skillets (and the TITUM nonstick line) — Nobody has measured this

Hestan says: “The innovative TITUM® nonstick cooking surface is reinforced with diamond and titanium particles, delivering reliable nonstick cookware that’s 22x more durable than conventional nonstick, standing up to the rigors of professional-grade cooking while cleaning effortlessly.”

their own page · read 2026-09-26

UNSUPPORTED — we went and checked; here is what we found · checkable only by a new independent measurement

Bottom line. Hestan says TITUM is 22x more durable than conventional nonstick. It publishes no test, names no comparison pan, and one of its own pages says 20x. Its lifetime warranty excludes normal wear.

What would settle it: A standardised abrasion and release test of new TITUM Fusion pans alongside an ordinary PTFE nonstick pan, under the same load and cycle count, reporting cycles to failure for each. Hestan would need to name the comparison pan the 22x is measured against.
Estimated cost: not yet priced

Evidence

  • our own desk check — mixed, we are not a neutral party here

    https://hestanculinary.com/pages/titum-story

    The product page and Hestan's two TITUM pages read in full: 22x three ways, 20x once, and no test behind either.

    The baseline changes from sentence to sentence (conventional, other, ordinary, standard nonstick), and on one page the number itself changes, from 22 to 20. A figure that came from a test would normally arrive with one number, one baseline and a method. This one arrives with several of the first two and none of the third.

  • document — mixed, funded by Hestan Culinary

    https://hestanculinary.com/pages/warranty

    Hestan's US lifetime warranty, read in full: it covers manufacturing defects and excludes normal wear and tear.

    The product page lists 'Lifetime Warranty' beside '22x More Durable', and a reader could take one as backing the other. The warranty covers defects, not wear, which is the thing the 22x is about.

Hestan uses '22x' across its TITUM pages, and each time the comparison is to something different: conventional nonstick, other nonstick, ordinary nonstick, standard nonstick. On the original TITUM page, a section headed '22x More Durable' goes on to say the coating is '20x more resilient'. We found no test on Hestan's site that produced either number.

Hestan is open about what the coating is. The product page says TITUM Fusion is a PTFE-based nonstick, reinforced with diamond and titanium particles. So the claim is that this PTFE coating outlasts other PTFE coatings 22-fold, and that is a claim a wear test could settle. The 'Lifetime Warranty' printed beside it does not settle it: it covers manufacturing defects and excludes normal wear and tear.

How we checked

  • our own desk check — hestanculinary.com

    Read on 2026-09-26 in the HTML the server sends. The product page says '22x longer-lasting performance', '22x More Durable vs. Other Nonstick' and '22x more durable than conventional nonstick'. The TITUM Fusion page says '22x more durable than other nonstick'. The original TITUM page heads a section '22x More Durable' and says, in the same section, 'TITUM®Nonstick is 20x more resilient than standard nonstick.' None of the pages describes a test, a method or a comparison pan. The product page also states: 'Hestan TITUM® Fusion features a PTFE-based nonstick finish'. We read Hestan's FAQ page, which does not mention the figure, and our sweep of Hestan's site (1,905 addresses in its sitemap, 31 pages fetched) found no durability test.

  • document — hestanculinary.com

    Read on 2026-09-26. Each piece 'is warranted to the original purchaser to be free from manufacturing defects in materials and workmanship under normal use for the lifetime of the cookware.' It 'does not cover damage caused by accident, misuse, abuse, normal wear and tear, or cosmetic conditions, including minor scratches, scuffs, surface markings'. Misuse that voids it includes 'sharp metal utensil or appliance damage on nonstick product surfaces' and 'using oven cleansers or scouring pads'.
