Made In Stainless Clad cookware (12-inch Stainless Clad frying pan page) — Holds up — but only on the seller's own evidence

Made In says: “All Stainless Clad pieces are made without: PFAS PFOA PTFE Lead Cadmium BPA Third-Party Tested For Safety:”

their own page · read 2026-09-26

SUPPORTED — evidence exists; nobody independent has measured the product · checkable by reading a document

Bottom line. Made In says its stainless pans are made without PFAS, PFOA, PTFE, lead, cadmium and BPA. A lab report it publishes finds none of 30 PFAS or 3 bisphenols. It does not test lead or cadmium.

Evidence

  • measurement — supports, funded by Made In

    https://app.lightlabs.com/comprehensive-testing-report/69/1623

    Light Labs' report on Made In's Stainless Clad pans: 30 PFAS each below a stated limit, and 3 bisphenols not detected. No lead, cadmium or PTFE test.

    This is a real, readable report, which puts it ahead of most cookware claims on this page. It covers three of the six things in the claim: PFAS, PFOA and BPA. The numbers are stated limits, not bare 'passes'. What the report cannot tell a reader is what the parts per billion are parts of: the pan's surface, a rinse, or something else. It also does not say whether 'within limit set by Made In' means anything more than 'not found'.

  • our own desk check — mixed, we are not a neutral party here

    https://madeincookware.com/products/stainless-steel-frying-pan/12-inch

    The product page lists six things the pans are made without, then 'Third-Party Tested For Safety' with the lab panel. The panel tests three of the six.

    Laid out this way, the page reads as if all six had been tested. Lead and cadmium were not, in the report we can read. PTFE is not on the list either, but a stainless pan has no coating for it to be in, and Made In's own disclosure puts its PTFE in the nonstick line.

This mostly checks out, and Made In makes it checkable. The lab report is one click from the product page, it is served from the lab's own site, and it gives a number for every PFAS result instead of a bare 'pass'. For an uncoated stainless pan, finding no PFAS is what you would expect, and the report confirms it.

The page promises a little more than the report shows. It lists six things, then says 'Third-Party Tested For Safety', but the report covers three of them. Lead and cadmium are not in it. They are not usual ingredients of stainless steel, so the claim is plausible, but it is Made In's word rather than a result. The report also leaves out what exactly was sampled, and Made In paid for it.

How we checked

  • measurement — app.lightlabs.com

    Read on 2026-09-26 from the lab's own domain, in the HTML its server sends. Product 'Stainless Clad', sample 'Stainless Clad Pans'. Two groups, 33 results, counted one by one. Bisphenols (last tested 19 September 2025): bisphenol A, F and S, each 'Not detected'. PFAS (last tested 14 February 2025): 30 compounds including PFOA (< 0.02 parts per billion) and PFOS (< 0.007), each reported below a limit between 0.007 and 0.1 parts per billion. Each result carries the note 'Substance within limit set by Made In Cookware'. The report says: 'Results reflect laboratory analysis of submitted samples at the time of testing as reported by the testing laboratory.' It does not say what the sample was or how it was prepared, or name a method.

  • our own desk check — madeincookware.com

    Read on 2026-09-26 in the HTML the server sends. Under 'Safety & Health' the page lists PFAS, PFOA, PTFE, lead, cadmium and BPA, followed by 'Third-Party Tested For Safety:' and a Light Labs panel. The panel is chosen by the page's address, which matches the Stainless Clad report above (not the nonstick or ceramic reports Made In also publishes). Made In's California disclosure page names PTFE, FEP and PFA for its nonstick line only, and names no chemicals for Stainless Clad.
