Made In ProCoat Non Stick Frying Pan (10-inch page; all Non Stick pieces) — Nothing published that a reader could check

Made In says: “All Non Stick pieces are made without: Lead Cadmium BPA Third-Party Tested For Safety:”

their own page · read 2026-09-28

UNSUPPORTED — we went and checked; here is what we found · checkable by reading a document

Bottom line. Made In says its nonstick is made without lead, cadmium and BPA, and shows a 'Third-Party Tested For Safety' lab report beneath. The report tests 30 PFAS chemicals and none of those three.

Evidence

  • measurement — mixed, funded by Made In

    https://app.lightlabs.com/comprehensive-testing-report/69/1622

    Light Labs' ProCoat Non Stick report: 30 PFAS, each below a limit in parts per billion. It has no lead, cadmium or BPA result.

    The report is real, readable and one click from the product page. It is simply about something else. The three things the page lists right above 'Third-Party Tested For Safety' are lead, cadmium and BPA, and the report tests none of them. What it does test is 30 small PFAS molecules, which is a fair thing to check in a pan whose coating is itself made of PFAS polymers (see Made In's own disclosure below), but it is not what the badge sits under.

  • our own desk check — mixed, we are not a neutral party here

    https://madeincookware.com/products/non-stick-frying-pan/10-inch-graphite

    The product page lists three things the nonstick is made without, lead, cadmium and BPA, then 'Third-Party Tested For Safety:' and the lab panel.

    Laid out this way, a reader would take the three items above the badge as the things that were tested. None of them were. The page also never says what the coating is, so the one list of chemicals on it is a list of things the pan does not contain.

  • document — mixed, funded by Made In

    https://madeincookware.com/pages/AB-1200

    Made In's California chemical disclosure names three fluoropolymers intentionally added to its nonstick line: PTFE, FEP and PFA.

    This is why the product page's 'made without' list stops at lead, cadmium and BPA: the coating is PTFE, and California's law counts PTFE as a PFAS. It also explains what the lab report is for. A PFAS panel on a PTFE pan checks for leftover small PFAS molecules such as PFOA, not for the coating itself.

Made In publishes a readable third-party lab report for its ProCoat nonstick, linked from the product page, and it finds none of 30 PFAS chemicals above small stated limits. Many brands on this page publish nothing at all.

The issue is placement. The page lists three things the nonstick is made without, lead, cadmium and BPA, and puts 'Third-Party Tested For Safety' directly under them. The report behind that badge does not test any of the three. It tests PFAS, which makes sense for this pan: Made In's own California disclosure says the coating is PTFE, with two related fluoropolymers, and a PFAS panel checks for leftover small molecules like PFOA. But a reader of the product page is shown a list of three chemicals and a testing badge, and would reasonably think one backs the other.

None of this suggests lead, cadmium or BPA is in the pan. They are not expected in a PTFE-coated stainless pan. It means that for those three, the published testing says nothing, and Made In's word is the only evidence.

How we checked

  • measurement — app.lightlabs.com

    Read on 2026-09-28 from the lab's own domain, in the HTML its server sends. Product 'ProCoat Non Stick', variant 'Procoat Nonstick'. One test group, PFAS, 'Last tested Feb 14, 2025': 30 compounds, counted one by one, each reported below a limit of 0.1, 0.4 or 3 parts per billion, including PFOA (< 0.4) and PFOS (< 0.1). Each carries the note 'Substance within limit set by Made In Cookware'. There is no metals, bisphenol or fluoropolymer group; the words lead, cadmium, BPA and PTFE do not appear. The report does not say what the parts per billion are measured in, describe the sample or name a method. It is signed 'Results approved by Lev Spivak-Birndorf, Lab Director', marked 'Accredited lab', and says: 'Results reflect laboratory analysis of submitted samples at the time of testing as reported by the testing laboratory.' The page's panel is chosen by the page's address; 'non-stick-frying-pan' is one of this report's matching addresses in Light Labs' own product feed for Made In (product 1622, 30 tests, 30 passed).

  • our own desk check — madeincookware.com

    Read on 2026-09-27 and again on 2026-09-28 in the HTML the server sends. The 'ProCoat Non Stick Frying Pan' ($149, 10 inch) has a 'Safety & Health' block: 'All Non Stick pieces are made without:' followed by 'Lead', 'Cadmium', 'BPA', then 'Third-Party Tested For Safety:' and the Light Labs panel. The page does not name the coating. Internet Archive captures of the page show the ProCoat name from January 2025, before the lab test; the 'Third-Party Tested' wording and the Light Labs panel are absent from the captures of January, May and August 2025 and present in the one of 17 November 2025.

  • document — madeincookware.com

    Read on 2026-09-24 in the HTML the server sends (the page cited by our 'FDA-approved' record for the same pan). It says: 'This disclosure is being provided to comply with the AB 1200 regulations: Our Non Stick Products - Non Stick Frying Pan, Non Stick Saute Pan, Non Stick Saucepan, Non Stick Rondeau, Non Stick Saucier, Non Stick Stock Pot and non stick products contained in sets - contain the following intentionally added chemicals: polytetrafluoroethylene (PTFE), fluorinated ethylene and propylene (FEP), and perfluoroalkoxy alkane polymer (PFA).'
