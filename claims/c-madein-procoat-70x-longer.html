Made In ProCoat Non Stick Frying Pan (and the ProCoat nonstick line) — Nobody has measured this

Made In says: “Say goodbye to non stick that needs to be replaced every 6 months. Our FDA-approved coating lasts up to 70x longer.”

their own page · read 2026-09-26

UNSUPPORTED — we went and checked; here is what we found · checkable only by a new independent measurement

Bottom line. Made In says its nonstick lasts up to 70x longer than pans that need replacing every 6 months, which works out to 35 years. It publishes no durability test, and its warranty covers 2 years and excludes wear.

What would settle it: A standardised abrasion and release test of new ProCoat pans alongside the comparison it names: an ordinary PTFE nonstick pan and a ceramic nonstick pan, abraded under the same load and cycle count, with food release measured at intervals until each fails. Only a side-by-side run could put a number on '70x', and the claim would also need its baseline pinned down.

Evidence

  • arithmetic — mixed, funded by Product Review (this publication)

    https://madeincookware.com/pages/warranty

    Made In's own numbers: 70 times a 6-month life is 35 years; its nonstick warranty is 2 years and excludes wear.

    A warranty is not a lifespan, and a 2-year warranty does not prove a pan wears out in 2 years. But the arithmetic shows how far the claim reaches past anything Made In itself stands behind: the multiple implies decades, and the warranty covers two years and excludes the ordinary wear the multiple is about. The page also measures the 70x against two different things, a 6-month nonstick pan in one place and 'ceramic' in another.

  • our own desk check — mixed, we are not a neutral party here

    https://app.lightlabs.com/comprehensive-testing-report/69/1622

    The only third-party report linked from the page, read in full: a PFAS chemical panel with no durability test.

    Made In does publish third-party testing for this pan, but that testing is about chemicals, not wear. Nothing published measures how long the coating lasts, against a 6-month pan, against ceramic, or against anything else.

'Up to 70x longer' is a claim only a wear test can settle, and Made In has not published one. The third-party report linked from the page tests for PFAS, not durability. So the number stands on Made In's word, and the page cannot keep its own comparison straight: the headline measures it against nonstick that 'needs to be replaced every 6 months', while the FAQ lower down says '70x longer than ceramic'.

Made In's own paperwork is the useful check. Seventy times six months is 35 years. Made In's warranty for the same pan is 2 years, and says that 'no coating will last a lifetime' and that normal wear is not covered. A warranty is not a prediction of lifespan, and the pan may well outlast cheaper ones. But a buyer weighing '70x' should know that the company making the claim does not insure it past year two.

How we checked

  • arithmetic — madeincookware.com

    Read on 2026-09-26. The product page's two sentences, taken together, put an ordinary nonstick pan at 6 months and this one at up to 70 times that: 0.5 years × 70 = 35 years. Made In's warranty page reads: 'Non Stick cookware comes with a 2-year limited warranty.' and 'Made In’s non stick coating is professional grade, but no coating will last a lifetime and normal depreciation from use is expected and not covered under warranty.' The same product page's FAQ gives a different baseline for the same multiple: 'The professional-grade coating is durable, last 70x longer than ceramic and is easy to clean.'

  • our own desk check — app.lightlabs.com

    Read on 2026-09-26. The page's 'Third-Party Tested For Safety' widget loads Light Labs' report for Made In's nonstick line (ProCoat Non Stick). It has one test category, PFAS, reporting each substance below a stated detection limit. It contains no abrasion, wear or lifespan test. Our sweep of Made In's site (2,487 addresses in its sitemap, 31 pages fetched) found no other test report bearing on durability.
