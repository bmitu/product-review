Made In Non Stick Frying Pan (and the Non Stick line) — Holds up

Made In says: “FDA-Approved Coating”

their own page · read 2026-09-26

SUPPORTED — evidence exists; nobody independent has measured the product · checkable by reading a document

Bottom line. Made In calls its nonstick coating FDA-approved. Its disclosed materials, PTFE, FEP and PFA, are resins an FDA regulation clears for food contact, so that holds. The product page never says the coating is PTFE.

Evidence

  • document — supports, independent

    https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/chapter-I/subchapter-B/part-177/subpart-B/section-177.1550

    The FDA regulation on perfluorocarbon resins in food-contact articles, read from the official electronic Code of Federal Regulations.

    The regulation says these resins 'may be safely used as articles or components of articles intended to contact food', subject to limits on what can be extracted from them. All three chemicals Made In discloses for its nonstick coating, PTFE, FEP and PFA, are resins this regulation covers. That is what 'FDA-approved' can truthfully mean here. The FDA has cleared the materials by regulation. It has not examined or approved Made In's coating as a product, because it does not do that for any pan.

  • document — supports, funded by Made In

    https://madeincookware.com/pages/AB-1200

    Made In's California chemical disclosure, read in full: it names the three fluoropolymers intentionally added to its nonstick line.

    This is what connects the claim to the regulation. The product page never says what the coating is made of. This page does, and those are the materials the regulation covers.

  • our own desk check — mixed, we are not a neutral party here

    https://madeincookware.com/products/non-stick-frying-pan/10-inch-graphite

    The nonstick frying pan's product page read in full: 'FDA-approved' appears five times, and the words PTFE, PFAS and PFOA not at all.

    The claim holds up, but notice what the page does with it. 'FDA-approved' is the only thing a shopper is told about the coating's chemistry. Someone searching for a pan without PTFE would not learn from this page that this one has it. They would need to find the California disclosure, one footer link away.

Supported, and worth reading closely anyway.

The FDA does not approve pans, or coatings as finished products. What it does is write regulations saying which materials may be used in contact with food, and under what limits. For nonstick coatings the relevant one covers PTFE and two related fluoropolymers, FEP and PFA, which are exactly the three chemicals Made In's own California disclosure lists for its nonstick line. 'FDA-approved' is shorthand for that, and it is fair shorthand: a regulation does permit these materials. We have not checked whether Made In's coating meets the regulation's extraction limits, which would take a lab.

The more useful finding for a shopper is what the phrase stands in for. On the product page, 'FDA-approved' is the entire description of the coating's chemistry. The words PTFE and PFAS do not appear on it. Made In's own disclosure, one link away, says the coating is PTFE. For a buyer who is fine with PTFE, nothing here is wrong. For a buyer trying to avoid it, the product page tells them nothing they need to know.

How we checked

  • document — ecfr.gov

    Read on 2026-09-26 from the eCFR's rendering service for 21 CFR 177.1550, 'Perfluorocarbon resins'. Paragraph (a) defines the resins covered: those made by polymerising tetrafluoroethylene and hexafluoropropylene, which takes in PTFE and FEP, and by copolymerising perfluoropropylvinylether with tetrafluoroethylene, which is PFA.

  • document — madeincookware.com

    Read on 2026-09-26 in the HTML the server sends. It states that the nonstick frying pan, sauté pan, saucepan, rondeau, saucier, stock pot and nonstick pieces in sets 'contain the following intentionally added chemicals: polytetrafluoroethylene (PTFE), fluorinated ethylene and propylene (FEP), and perfluoroalkoxy alkane polymer (PFA).'

  • our own desk check — madeincookware.com

    Read on 2026-09-26 in the HTML the server sends. The page carries 'FDA-Approved Coating' in its feature list, 'FDA-approved Non Stick' in its description, 'Our FDA-approved coating lasts up to 70x longer' under 'Crafted to Last', and 'a high quality, FDA-approved Non Stick surface' in its FAQ. A text search of the page for PTFE, PFAS and PFOA finds none. The disclosure page is linked from the site footer.
