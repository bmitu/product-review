Cuisinart FusionPlus Hybrid Stainless Steel 12-inch Skillet (FPS22-30H) — Holds up — but only on the seller's own evidence

Cuisinart says: “PFAS-free* ceramic nonstick interior offers highly durable, scratch-resistant cooking surface … * No intentionally added PFAS”

their own page · read 2026-09-27

SUPPORTED — evidence exists; nobody independent has measured the product · checkable by reading a document

Bottom line. Cuisinart says FusionPlus has 'no intentionally added PFAS'. Its own disclosure for the pan lists nothing fluorinated. That holds, and the footnote says exactly what the paperwork can show.

Evidence

  • document — supports, funded by Cuisinart

    https://www.cuisinart.com/chemical-disclosure

    Cuisinart's disclosure record for FPS22-30H, read in full: nine substances, none fluorinated.

    The claim is carefully worded: its footnote says 'No intentionally added PFAS', which is exactly what a state disclosure can check, since the law asks for chemicals added on purpose. Cuisinart's record for this pan lists stainless-steel and aluminum metals and nothing fluorinated.

  • our own desk check — supports, we are not a neutral party here

    https://www.cuisinart.com/fusionplus-hybrid-stainless-steel-12-skillet-with-helper/FPS22-30H.html

    The FusionPlus 12-inch product page read in full: the claim and its footnote are live; no test report is linked.

    The footnote narrows the claim to what was put in, not what a test might find, and says so on the page. That is the honest version of a PFAS-free claim.

This checks out. The claim is qualified by its own footnote, 'No intentionally added PFAS', which is the question a state chemical disclosure answers. Cuisinart's record for this pan lists nine substances, all metals, and none of them PFAS, while its record for an older conventional nonstick pan names three fluoropolymers.

As with every disclosure, this describes what goes into the pan, not a test of what comes out, and we could not sweep Cuisinart's site for a published test report because it blocks automated reading.
