Hybrid (stainless over nonstick)

What a hybrid (stainless over nonstick) cooking surface is and what is known about it, one sourced sentence at a time; then every product line we list with this surface, and the claims we have checked about them. We do not say which to buy.

3 product lines from 3 brands. 4 checked claims. See every brand.

How we decide a line's surface: The cooking surfaces of the frying pans and skillets the brand sells, in the brand's own words. We say PTFE or ceramic only where the brand's own page does (Teflon, a trademark for PTFE coatings, counts as naming PTFE); otherwise the coating is 'not named'.

What it is and what is known

  1. HexClad says the nonstick in the valleys between the raised steel hexagons of its Hybrid pans is its own TerraBond ceramic coating, which it says is free from PTFE.
    Source: HexClad, “10" Hybrid Pan” (the brand's own words, not independent), read 2026-09-30.
    HexClad's Hybrid line is listed on this page.
    Their words
    “Protected between the hexagonal steel peaks are nonstick valleys containing our proprietary TerraBond™ ceramic nonstick coating, which is free from PTFE and forever chemicals”
  2. GreenPan describes its Spectra pans as a hybrid of 3-ply stainless steel and its ceramic nonstick.
    Source: GreenPan, “Spectra Hybrid Ceramic Nonstick 8" Frypan” (the brand's own words, not independent), read 2026-09-30.
    GreenPan's Spectra line is listed on this page.
    Their words
    “Spectra is the best of hybrid cooking, combining the strength and responsiveness of 3-ply stainless steel with the ease of our best ceramic nonstick.”
  3. T-fal's product page for its HybridMesh frying pan says the pan contains PTFE.
    Source: T-fal, “HybridMesh Stainless Steel 10.5 inch Frypan” (the brand's own words, not independent), read 2026-09-30.
    T-fal's HybridMesh line is listed on this page.
    Their words
    “This product contains PTFE for its nonstick properties.” (Specifications table, 'Properties' row. The same page describes the pan as blending 'exposed stainless steel for superior searing power and the convenience of our non-stick coating'.)
  4. Researchers describe the coatings sold as 'ceramic' nonstick as silica particles held in a siloxane, a silicone-type binder.
    Source: RSC Advances (Royal Society of Chemistry), 2026, via PubMed Central, “Non-stick performance of polymethylsilsesquioxane thin films synthesized by sol–gel spray coating” (peer-reviewed study), read 2026-09-30.
    Their words
    “These coatings consist of silica particles embedded in a siloxane matrix” (Introduction; the sentence directly follows 'They are often marketed as non-stick “ceramic coatings”.')
