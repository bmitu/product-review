How we decide a line's surface: The cooking surfaces of the frying pans and skillets the brand sells, in the brand's own words. We say PTFE or ceramic only where the brand's own page does (Teflon, a trademark for PTFE coatings, counts as naming PTFE); otherwise the coating is 'not named'.

What it is and what is known

  1. As a metal, titanium is known for resisting corrosion and for being strong for its weight.
    Source: U.S. Geological Survey, “Titanium Statistics and Information” (government research), read 2026-09-30.
    Their words
    “As a metal, titanium is well known for corrosion resistance and for its high strength-to-weight ratio.”
  2. In air, titanium grows a protective film on its surface by itself, only a few nanometres thick.
    Source: npj Materials Degradation (Nature Portfolio), “Do titanium biomaterials get immediately and entirely repassivated? A perspective” (peer-reviewed study), read 2026-09-30.
    Their words
    “under atmospheric conditions, a robust protective film can develop spontaneously on the surface of titanium objects. Its thickness is in the range of a few nanometers” (section 'Passivity of titanium based materials')
  3. Our Place describes the inside of its Titanium Pro pan as an uncoated titanium surface, engineered to imitate the way water beads off a lotus leaf.
    Source: Our Place, “Titanium Always Pan Pro” (the brand's own words, not independent), read 2026-09-30.
    Our Place's Titanium Pro line is listed on this page.
    Their words
    “Our no-coating NoCo® titanium interior offers a patented nonstick technology engineered to mimic the Lotus Effect”
  4. Hestan says its NanoBond pans bond thousands of titanium nano-layers onto 18/10 stainless steel: a titanium surface on a stainless pan.
    Source: Hestan Culinary, “NanoBond Titanium Skillet” (the brand's own words, not independent), read 2026-09-30.
    Hestan's NanoBond line is listed on this page.
    Their words
    “bonds thousands of titanium nano-layers to the 18/10 stainless steel construction”
  5. T-fal describes the coating on its Ultimate Hard Anodized frypans as a 'titanium non-stick coating'.
    Source: T-fal, “Ultimate Hard Anodized Non-Stick Cookware 2 Piece Frypan Set” (the brand's own words, not independent), read 2026-09-30.
    Their words
    “Titanium non-stick coating keeps food sliding smoothly”
  6. The same T-fal page says the product contains PTFE for its nonstick properties. On this pan, 'titanium' is the name of a PTFE coating, not a titanium cooking surface.
    Source: T-fal, “Ultimate Hard Anodized Non-Stick Cookware 2 Piece Frypan Set” (the brand's own words, not independent), read 2026-09-30.
    We list T-fal's Ultimate Hard Anodized line under PTFE nonstick, not here.
    Their words
    “This product contains PTFE for its nonstick properties.”
